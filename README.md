### Eva

**[Eva](https://github.com/Ilhe8l/eva)** is my personal voice assistant, and it runs entirely on my own computer. It's built on Deep Agents and LangGraph, uses faster-whisper to listen and Kokoro to speak, and asks before doing anything with side effects. When she can't do something yet, she writes a skill for it. She's named after EVE (EVA in Brazil) from WALL-E.
